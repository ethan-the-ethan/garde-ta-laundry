Hello! Thanks for checking out our website!
This is Garde ta Laundry, a website that allows you to see when the Thompson's Lane washers are in use from the comfort of your own room. Or the library. Or whatever.

Q. Why have you taped electronics to the washing machines?

Principally, they're just vibration detectors that report whether or not their respective machine is in use to our webserver. You can then access that data here.

Q. I just went to the washer and the website was wrong!

Ah! That's annoying. If you find this happening a lot, please contact us (see below)!

Q. Can I see the code?

Yes, it's on Github!
